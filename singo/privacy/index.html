Singo privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Singo is a music bingo game. The Singo app runs the game on your TV, and guests play along in the web browser on their phones over your home Wi-Fi. This policy explains what information Singo handles and where it goes. In short: the developer of Singo does not collect, receive or sell any of your data.

What stays on your TV

  • The game: the names guests type in, their bingo cards, their answers and the results. They are saved on the TV so a game survives a restart, and they are deleted when you start a new game or uninstall the app.
  • Settings: things like the countdown length and the music source.
  • Your Spotify login, if you connect Spotify: the Client ID of your own Spotify developer app and the login tokens Spotify issues. They are only used to talk to Spotify for you.

Guests' phones connect directly to the TV on your local network. Names and answers are shown to the other players in the room, as part of the game.

Spotify

If you choose to connect Spotify, Singo talks directly to Spotify from your TV to read your playlists, play songs on the speaker you pick, and look up song details. Spotify handles that information under Spotify's privacy policy. Singo does not send your Spotify data anywhere else.

When you log in to Spotify from your phone, Spotify sends the phone to a small page on GitHub Pages (mattthiasschedel.github.io), which passes the login straight back to your TV. That page stores nothing. The login code it passes on is useless without a secret that only your TV holds. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

You can play without Spotify at all by choosing "Play music myself instead".

What Singo does not do

  • No accounts with the developer, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • No data is sent to the developer or sold or shared with anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Singo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
